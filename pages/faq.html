What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a student management and information portal that provides access to student services, profiles, events, and other information.

How do I create an account?

Visit the registration page and complete the student registration form.

Go to Registration

How do I login?

Use your registered email address and password on the StudentHub login page.

Go to Login

How can I update my profile?

After logging in, open your profile and select the edit profile option.
