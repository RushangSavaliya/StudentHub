About StudentHub

StudentHub is a web-based student management portal developed to provide students and administrators with a centralized platform for accessing and managing student-related information.

Our Purpose

The main purpose of StudentHub is to simplify access to student services and provide an organized platform for managing student information and college events.

Portal Users

Students: Manage profiles, view events, and access student services.

Administrators: Manage student records, events, and portal content.
